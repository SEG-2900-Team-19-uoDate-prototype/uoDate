welcome to

Dating made simple, exclusively for GeeGees!


Designed by students, for students, uoDate is designed to help uOttawa students connect and find love right here in the heart of Canada's capital city. uoDate is a safe and easy-to-use platform for students to meet and date and that is free to use as well as select premium features.


uoDate is still in development, but you can keep up with our progress at our GitHub repository.



How it works

  • Create an account

    Sign up for a free account using your uOttawa email address and create a profile to get started.

  • Verify your account

    Verify your account using the verification link sent to your uOttawa email address.

  • Find your match

    Get started by making a profile and finding your perfect match right here on campus.




Try out our demo to see how uoDate works!
