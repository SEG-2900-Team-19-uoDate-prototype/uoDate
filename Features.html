Product Features

uoDate is designed to be a simple and easy-to-use dating platform for uOttawa students. Here are some of the features that make uoDate unique:


  • User-Friendly Interface

    Our intuitive design makes it easy for you to navigate and use uoDate without any hassle.

  • Organizing Matches by Interests

    uoDate allows you to organize your matches by uOttawa residence, major, shared interest, and more.

  • Secure Messaging

    We prioritize your privacy and security, ensuring that your personal messages and all media sent through the platform are protected.

  • Real-Time Updates

    Stay up-to-date with the latest features and improvements as we continuously work to enhance your uoDate experience.

We also provide special features for our dedicated Gold members.

  • Profile Boosts

    uoDate Gold members can boost their profiles to increase visibility and attract more matches. They can also organize what kinds of matches they want to boost.

  • See Who Liked You

    Gold members can see who has liked their profile, making it easier to connect with potential matches.

  • Ad-Free Experience

    Enjoy an uninterrupted experience on uoDate without any ads when you subscribe to uoDate Gold.
