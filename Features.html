Features

Key Features

  • Our platform is designed exclusively for current university students and university graduates.
  • Students from different universities can connect to share goals, advice, and build meaningful relationships.
  • Users can optionally share their location with trusted connections while maintaining full privacy controls..
  • Verify profiles through university email authentication.

Safety Features

  • Report and Block users
  • Users are able to control who can message them.
  • Optional location sharing with trusted users only.
  • Users can control who can view their profiles

Dating Features

  • Connect with students for across different universities in Canada
  • Match with other users based on common goals, shared interests
  • Create a profile that highlights academic interest, hobbies, and futures goals.
